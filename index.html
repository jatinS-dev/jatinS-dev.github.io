    From Sights to Insights: Towards Summarization of Multimodal Clinical Documents
    Jatin Avinash Salve and others
    Proceedings of the 62nd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL)This work represents a significant advancement in multimodal clinical NLP, with applications in healthcare documentation and medical decision support systems. , Aug 2024
    Abs Bib

    We present EDI-Summ, a novel multimodal summarization architecture that combines contextual and cross-attention mechanisms for clinical document summarization. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance with 0.81 factual recall and 3.51 clinical score, outperforming GPT-3.5 and GPT-4V on clinical summarization tasks. The system integrates visual and textual information from clinical documents to generate comprehensive summaries that maintain both factual accuracy and clinical relevance.
