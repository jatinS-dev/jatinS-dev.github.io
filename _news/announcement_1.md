🎉 **ACL 2024 Publication**: Our paper "From Sights to Insights: Towards Summarization of Multimodal Clinical Documents" has been accepted at the 62nd Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics (ACL 2024)! This work presents EDI-Summ, a novel multimodal summarization architecture that outperforms GPT-3.5 and GPT-4V on clinical summarization tasks.
